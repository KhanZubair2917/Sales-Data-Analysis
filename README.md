# sales data analytics

The project includes data organizing and presenting it in a comprehensible for growth of the company and make better decisions according to the data.

##  Dataset

Started with 305 Rows, cleaned down to 297 rows after removing the duplicates and outliers. it includes order details like product, category, region, price, and units sold. 7 columns to be exact. it includes realistic data quality issues(duplicates, missing value, outliers).

## What i did?

- Cleaned the data: handled missing value, removed duplicates, standardized the text.
- Analysed the revenue by category, region and product.
- Visualized findings for better understanding the data analysis.

## Key findings

We're making the most out of electronics and selling the best in north region. laptop is our most revenue generating product. we're missing region data for 15 orders which is not good, i'd recommend to investigate the data collection process for these 15 orders to prevent future gaps.
